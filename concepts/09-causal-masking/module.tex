\section{Causal masking}\label{sec:09}
\conceptbadge{09}

Autoregressive language modeling predicts the next token from previous tokens.
When training on a full sequence, the model sees all token embeddings at once
for efficiency. Causal masking enforces the correct constraint on that parallel computation:
position $i$ may attend only to positions $j \le i$.

\subsection*{The leakage problem}
Suppose the sequence is
\[
  x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots, x_T.
\]
At position $i$, the model is trained to predict $x_{i+1}$. If its attention
layer can read position $i+1$ or later, the answer is directly available in the
input. Training loss would look good while generation would fail, because at
generation time future tokens do not exist yet.

\subsection*{The mask}
For a length-$T$ sequence, the allowed attention pattern is lower triangular:
\[
  M_{ij} =
  \begin{cases}
    1 & \text{if } j \le i,\\
    0 & \text{if } j > i.
  \end{cases}
\]
In implementation, the mask is applied to attention logits before softmax. A
forbidden score is replaced with a very negative number, often written as
$-\infty$:
\[
  S_{ij} =
  \begin{cases}
    S_{ij} & \text{if } j \le i,\\
    -\infty & \text{if } j > i.
  \end{cases}
\]
Softmax turns those forbidden positions into probability zero.

\subsection*{No-leakage test}
A good causal attention implementation has a simple behavioral property: if you
change tokens after position $i$, the output at position $i$ must not change.
Later positions may depend on earlier ones, but earlier positions cannot depend
on later ones. This is exactly the property P5 checks.

\subsection*{Training and generation}
During training, causal masking lets the model process every position in a
sequence in parallel. During generation, the same model is called repeatedly:
feed the current context, sample or choose the next token, append it, and repeat.
The mask ensures the training computation matches that future-blind generation
setting.

\subsection*{Exercise connection}
The exercise focuses on the mask operation itself. Apply the mask before
softmax, not after; masking probabilities after softmax breaks normalization
unless you renormalize.
