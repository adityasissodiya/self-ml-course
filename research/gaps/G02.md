# G02 — Transitive cross-organizational delegation with attenuation

**Priority:** HIGH | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM-HIGH  
**Status:** narrowing  
**Feeds from:** Modules 02, 03, 05, 06  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

Zanzibar/ReBAC descendants model relations among principals and resources, but were not designed for runtime-composed agent delegation. RFC 8693 says nested `act` claims are informational and not used for access decisions; OAuth does not give holders a general scope-attenuation primitive.

## What is open

A model and enforcement path that expresses and verifies multi-hop, cross-organizational delegation with attenuation, revocation, provenance, and stakeholder consent.

## Why we are positioned for it

This is the thesis's multi-stakeholder access-control problem instantiated in DPP, circular-economy, and public-safety workflows.
