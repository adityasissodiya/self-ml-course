# G07 — DID/VC agent identity for DPP access

**Priority:** MEDIUM | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 03, 05, 07  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

DPP architectures use role-based selective disclosure and decentralized data planes; W3C DID v1.1 and VC Data Model v2.0 supply identity/credential primitives. The proposal notes MCP-I's March 2026 DIF donation and adoption/latency concerns.

## What is open

Agent-mediated DPP access in which agents present VCs for scoped, attenuated, revocable authority across registry and decentralized storage.

## Why we are positioned for it

This has maximum alignment with DPP, RemaNet, COP-PILOT, and the thesis, providing the clearest differentiator from generic ML-security work.

Medium if a narrow DPP flow is used; throughput and revocation can expand scope rapidly.
