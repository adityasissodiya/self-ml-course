# G10 — Governance versus protocol layer

**Priority:** MEDIUM | **Difficulty:** LOW-MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 01, 02  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

AAIF governance addresses stewardship, while MCP/A2A/ACP do not express which agent may read which data. arXiv 2606.31498 catalogues governance gaps in the protocols.

## What is open

A defensible allocation of cross-organizational authorization responsibilities among protocol, policy overlay, and registry.

## Why we are positioned for it

The DPP registry/decentralized-storage split supplies a concrete governance boundary. This is the fastest fallback paper if implementation exceeds the timebox.
