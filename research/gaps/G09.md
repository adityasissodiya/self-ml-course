# G09 — Revocation and break-glass across organizations

**Priority:** MEDIUM | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM-HIGH  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 03, 04, 05, 07  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

Short-lived SVIDs and OAuth tokens bound exposure, but systematic revocation of in-flight, multi-hop delegation is thin. Public-safety workflows also need emergency grants with after-the-fact accountability.

## What is open

Revocation propagation and break-glass semantics across organizations, including what happens to derived authority and running actions.

## Why we are positioned for it

Public-safety incident response is a thesis-native scenario with clear stakeholders and accountability requirements.

## Concurrent work

| Work | Venue/date | Overlap | Our delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPIFFE short-lived SVID practice | Current standard/ecosystem | Limits credential lifetime | Delegation-derived authority, in-flight action, emergency override |
| HDP | 2026 preprint | Provenance | Revocation decision and break-glass enforcement |

## Difficulty assessment

Medium-high because distributed revocation has consistency, availability, and safety tradeoffs.
