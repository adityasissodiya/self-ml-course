# G05 — Safety-problem decidability for agent delegation

**Priority:** MEDIUM | **Difficulty:** HIGH  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 05, 06  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

General HRU safety is undecidable; take-grant and useful trust-management fragments admit efficient analysis. The Berkeley/ETH formalization preprint is an early bridge to LLM agents.

## What is open

Whether runtime-composed delegation and revocation fall in a decidable fragment, and which restrictions retain useful expressiveness.

## Why we are positioned for it

The Module 05 model supplies a concrete system to abstract, though this path needs formal-methods supervision.

High-risk, high-reward. Do not promise a decidability theorem inside the course without specialist support.
