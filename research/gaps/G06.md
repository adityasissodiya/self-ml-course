# G06 — Workload identity versus on-behalf-of identity

**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 01, 03  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

SPIFFE authenticates the agent workload. The user-on-whose-behalf identity and delegated authority are separate. WIMSE/OAuth proposals and the “workload identity trap” expose the boundary.

## What is open

A decision framework for when workload identity suffices and a clean, verifiable binding when user delegation is also required.

## Why we are positioned for it

The two-organization lab makes both identity layers observable and testable.

## Concurrent work

| Work | Venue/date | Overlap | Our delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `draft-klrc-aiagent-auth` | IETF draft | WIMSE + OAuth for agents | Decision criteria and DPP-specific binding tests |
| AIP / AI Identity preprints | 2026 | Agent identity and delegation protocols | Compare against workload identity and deployed enforcement |

## Difficulty assessment

Medium: a rigorous decision framework is feasible; a universal identity protocol is not.
