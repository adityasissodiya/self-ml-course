# G03 — Capability attenuation versus the OAuth standards track

**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 03, 05, 07  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

Macaroons and biscuits support offline-verifiable holder attenuation. RFC 8693/OAuth token exchange relies on an authorization server to issue narrowed tokens. The attenuating-agent-tokens Internet-Draft is unadopted as recorded by the proposal; that status must be rechecked before publication.

## What is open

A principled interoperability story for MCP/A2A agent chains and empirical comparison of security properties, latency, revocation, and auditability.

## Why we are positioned for it

The mechanism is prototype-sized and connects identity, enforcement, and evaluation modules.

## Difficulty assessment

Medium: implementation is tractable; rigorous revocation and interoperability claims are the hard part.
