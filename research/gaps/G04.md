# G04 — Delegation-chain accountability and provenance

**Priority:** HIGH | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 03, 04, 07  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

A2A signs Agent Cards, proving an identity binding rather than delegated authority. HDP proposes append-only delegation provenance. RFC 8693 retains the current actor for decisions, while OAuth identity chaining targets multi-hop representation.

## What is open

Verifiable end-to-end provenance across organizations that supports after-the-fact accountability without turning stale chain history into live authority.

## Why we are positioned for it

Public-safety break-glass workflows give a distinctive, accountability-heavy setting.

## Concurrent work

| Work | Venue/date | Overlap | Our delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| HDP, arXiv 2604.04522 | 2026 preprint | Append-only human delegation provenance | Cross-org enforcement and break-glass audit semantics |
| OAuth identity chaining | IETF draft | Multi-hop identity chain | Separate decision-relevant facts from audit-only history |

## Difficulty assessment

Medium if scoped to a trace format and verifier; harder if privacy-preserving provenance is required.
