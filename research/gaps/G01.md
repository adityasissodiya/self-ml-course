# G01 — Standardized benchmark for agent access-control enforcement

**Priority:** HIGH | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** open  
**Feeds from:** Modules 04, 05, 07  
**Last verified:** 2026-09-05

## What is established

AgentDojo, InjecAgent, ASB, AgentHarm, ToolEmu, OpenAgentSafety, and τ-bench measure injection, misuse, safety, or policy adherence. Progent, MiniScope, and SEAgent use bespoke datasets; their design choices expose the lack of common least-privilege evaluation.

## What is open

A shared realistic benchmark for unauthorized access, over-privilege, confused-deputy resistance, revocation, and enforcement latency under an explicit delegation threat model.

## Why we are positioned for it

The student's evaluation and engineering experience can supply a reproducible protocol, with DPP and public-safety scenarios providing realistic task sources.

## Concurrent work

| Work | Venue/date | Overlap | Our delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Progent / MiniScope / SEAgent | 2025–2026 | Bespoke least-privilege or case-study tasks | Shared delegation model, policy ground truth, cross-org cases |
| IntellAgent tasks | Reported in 2025 eval survey | RBAC-aware evaluation | Multi-hop attenuation and revocation, not only RBAC adherence |

## Difficulty assessment

Medium: buildable within the course, but validity depends on scenario diversity and defensible ground truth.
