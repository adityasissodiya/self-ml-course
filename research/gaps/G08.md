**Priority:** MEDIUM | **Difficulty:** MEDIUM

## What is established

OWASP ASI01–ASI10, T01–T17, and MAESTRO are useful checklists. The zero-trust architecture preprint arXiv 2508.12259 says MAESTRO lacks mathematical formalism for rigorous analysis.

## What is open

A mapping from identity/privilege abuse and insecure inter-agent communication to explicit authorization properties and mechanisms.

## Why we are positioned for it

The course combines access-control theory with concrete threat-model and policy labs.
