\sheet{Workshop 2 activity: break the policy}{Pairs, 30 minutes. Agentic AI Security, Identity, and Access Control.}

\textbf{The model, in short.} Each passport field has a read relation. Members of stakeholder organizations related to the passport inherit it. Agents get access only through \texttt{delegate\_<field>} tuples.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Who may read it} \\
\midrule
identity & everyone (public) \\
materials & members of the owner, recycler, or regulator organizations; delegated agents \\
health & members of the owner, recycler, or repairer organizations; delegated agents \\
repair & members of the owner or repairer organizations; delegated agents \\
suppliers & members of the owner organization; delegated agents \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\medskip
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
People & \texttt{user:alice@recycler}, \texttt{user:carol@manufacturer}, \texttt{user:mallory@broker} \\
Agents & \texttt{agent:recycler/orchestrator}, \texttt{agent:lab/xrf-analyzer}, \texttt{agent:broker/harvester}, \texttt{agent:regulator/incident-response} \\
Passports & \texttt{passport:battery-001} (manufacturer), \texttt{passport:battery-002} (othermaker) \\
Tuples & \texttt{[object, relation, user]}, e.g.\ \texttt{["organization:recycler", "member", "user:alice@recycler"]} \\
\end{tabularx}
