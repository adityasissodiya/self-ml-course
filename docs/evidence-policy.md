# Evidence policy

Last verified: 2026-09-05

## Evidence tiers

| Tag | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| `[A]` | Peer-reviewed research, or a preprint explicitly labelled as not yet peer-reviewed | Evidence, with venue/status stated |
| `[B]` | Standard, specification, RFC, or normative protocol document | Claims about defined behavior and requirements |
| `[C]` | Community, government-framework, or industry/grey literature | Motivation and implementation context only |

Each course item also carries `[LB]` (load-bearing) or `[BG]` (background). Every claim in the paper artifact should be labelled in working notes as **established (A/B)**, **reasonable inference**, or **speculation/open**.

## Access tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FREE` | Open access without an account |
| `FREE-ACCT` | Free with registration/login |
| `PAID` | Payment required; record price/currency and a firm purchase recommendation |
| `INST` | Institutional access, such as LTU/ACM/IEEE |
| `GATED` | Form or paywall blocks verification |

Unreachable resources remain in place and are labelled `⚠️ UNVERIFIED — checked YYYY-MM-DD`. A gated 403 is expected and reported separately by the link checker.

## Grey-literature rule

Every `[C]` reading row carries: `Use: motivation only — not citable as evidence`. The IBM/Ponemon figures are vendor-sponsored, self-reported results from a convenience panel of breached organizations; “proper AI access controls” is not operationalized, IBM is a security vendor, and the 2026 primary report is gated. The figures may motivate the work but may not establish necessity or efficacy.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Citation resolves and is the primary source where possible.
- [ ] Tier, priority, access, venue/status, and verification date are recorded.
- [ ] Preprints are not described as peer-reviewed.
- [ ] A claim does not exceed what its source supports.
- [ ] Concurrent work and negative evidence are retained.
